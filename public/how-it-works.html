How FindMyPlumber works

Short version: you call, we route you to a licensed local plumbing professional, they do the work. Here is the long version, including the parts most referral sites leave out.

(831) 532-6042

Calls may be routed to a network of licensed local plumbing professionals.

Step by step

The three steps

Call or search

Dial (831) 532-6042 or open your city page. The city pages list the specific neighborhoods, ZIP codes and nearby towns each one covers, so you can check whether your address is in scope before you call.

Get connected to a licensed local pro

We ask for your location, the problem, and whether the water is shut off. Then we route the call to an independent licensed plumbing professional who serves that area and is available.

They quote and fix it

The plumber assesses the job, gives you a price, and does the work if you want them to. Your contract, invoice and any warranty are with that plumber.

What we are

FindMyPlumber is a lead generation and call routing service. We publish information about plumbing problems in specific Central Coast cities and operate a phone line that connects callers to plumbing professionals. That is the entire scope of what we do.

What we are not

We are not a plumbing company. We do not hold a contractor licence, we do not employ plumbers, we do not own the vans, we do not set prices, and we do not perform or supervise any plumbing work. Nothing on this site should be read as a contractor offering to do work for you.

How the phone number works

The number shown on this site, (831) 532-6042, is a tracked number. When the page loads, a call tracking platform called Ringba may replace the number you see with another number from its pool so that the call can be attributed to the page you were reading and routed correctly. If that script does not load, the number above still works and still routes.

Routing and tracking happen on the phone number. We do not use that mechanism to collect your browsing history, and the details are set out in the privacy policy.

How we make money

Plumbing businesses pay us when they receive a qualified call from this site. This is called a pay-per-call arrangement. You are never charged anything by FindMyPlumber — not for the call, not for the referral, and not as a percentage of the work.

That arrangement creates an obvious incentive, so here is how we handle it: we do not rank or route based on which plumber pays the most, we do not publish invented reviews to make the site look more established than it is, and we tell you when nobody serving your area is available rather than holding your call.

What we deliberately do not claim

You will not find any of the following on this site, because we cannot verify them:

  • Customer testimonials, star ratings or review counts
  • A promised response time or arrival window
  • A satisfaction percentage or number of jobs completed
  • A founding year, a business address, or a named owner of a plumbing company
  • A count of plumbers "in our network" in any given city

If we ever have real, sourced figures for any of those, we will publish them with the source attached.

What happens to your information

Enough to route the call: your location, the nature of the problem, and your phone number if you give it. Call tracking software also records technical session data about the page you called from. If call recording is enabled on the campaign, your call may be recorded — see the privacy policy for the disclosure and your options.

If something goes wrong

Your contract is with the plumber you hire. If a referred plumber does poor work, that dispute is between you and that business, and the California Contractors State License Board is the body that handles contractor complaints. That said, if a referral went badly wrong we want to know: use the contact page and we will look into it and stop sending calls to that business if it is warranted.
