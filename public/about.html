About FindMyPlumber

A free referral service for people on California's Central Coast who need a plumber and would rather talk to one than fill in a form.

What this site is

FindMyPlumber publishes practical, city-specific information about plumbing problems and connects callers with independent, licensed local plumbing professionals. It launched with 18 city pages across 6 Central Coast counties, from Gilroy and Hollister in the north down through the Salinas Valley and Monterey Peninsula to Santa Barbara in the south.

It is a referral and call routing service. It is not a plumbing company. We do not hold a contractor licence, employ plumbers, or perform or supervise plumbing work.

Why it exists

Searching for a plumber usually produces one of two things: a paid directory stuffed with invented five-star reviews, or a list of national lead-generation forms that sell your phone number to whoever bids on it. Neither is much use at eleven at night with water on the kitchen floor.

This site does one narrow thing instead. It tells you honestly what goes wrong with plumbing in your specific town — hard water in Santa Maria, pre-1970 clay and Orangeburg laterals in Santa Cruz, salt-air corrosion in Pacific Grove — and then gives you a phone number that reaches a licensed plumbing professional who serves your area.

How we make money

Plumbing businesses pay us per qualified call. That is the pay-per-call model, and it is the only revenue this site has. It costs you nothing to call or to be referred.

Editorial rules we hold ourselves to

No invented proof

No fake testimonials, no star ratings, no review counts, no satisfaction percentages, no jobs-completed figures. There is nothing to show yet, so nothing is shown.

No invented history

No founding year, no "family owned since", no business address, no named owner of a plumbing company that does not exist.

No invented numbers

Every figure on a city page comes from a public source — the U.S. Census Bureau, a water utility's published quality report, or a city or county program page — and the source is stated on the page.

No copied pages

Each city page is written about that city: real neighborhoods, real ZIP codes, and the plumbing issues that are genuinely common there. If we cannot write something true and specific about a place, we do not publish a page for it.

Coverage

The site is published in batches. Batch one is the 18 cities listed below. Additional cities are added over time, and each new page has to meet the same standard as the existing ones — which is why they do not all appear at once.

Monterey County: Salinas, Monterey, Seaside, Marina, Pacific Grove, Soledad, Greenfield

Santa Cruz County: Watsonville, Santa Cruz

San Benito County: Hollister

Santa Clara County: Gilroy

San Luis Obispo County: Paso Robles, Atascadero, San Luis Obispo, Pismo Beach, Morro Bay

Santa Barbara County: Santa Barbara, Santa Maria

Who to contact

For plumbing help, call (831) 532-6042. For anything about this website — a correction, a complaint about a referred plumber, a request to add your town — use the contact page or write to hello@findmyplumber.com.

Correction policy. If a factual statement on any page of this site is wrong, tell us and we will fix it or remove it. The local detail on these pages is researched, but it is researched by humans and it can be out of date.
